Purchase Requisition

How to raise a purchase requisition

  1. Choose the request date and whether the purchase is daily or weekly.
  2. Add each ingredient or supply, the quantity needed, and the estimated cost.
  3. Save the requisition. It starts as a draft that you can review.
  4. Submit it for approval. An authorised procurement approver will approve, return, or reject it.
  5. After approval, use Create PO to place the supplier order.
